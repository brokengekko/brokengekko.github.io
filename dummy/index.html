How to Use the Manakin

  • Posing Joints: Click and drag handles mapped to specific joints. Dragging the Elbow moves the Upper Arm. Dragging the Wrist moves the Lower Arm. Dragging the Hand moves the Wrist.
  • Torso & Head: Grab the upper-center Chest Peg to bend the waist. Grab the Head to pivot the neck.
  • Leg Inversion: Dragging lower legs (knees) left/right is naturally inverted for easier posing.
  • Exporting: Save the current pose as a tiny JSON file, or download a crisp PNG image to trace over in your art software.
