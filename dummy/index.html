How to Use Manakin Studio

Moving Joints: Click and drag the outermost extension of a joint (e.g., the wrist to move the forearm, or the ankle to move the lower leg) to pose the figure.

Swivel: Use the Swivel L/R buttons to rotate the entire platform by 15% (54 degrees).

Styles: Switch between Classic (wooden realistic), Pencil (light sketch lines), and Charcoal (heavy, dark shading) in real-time.

Save & Load: Download your pose as a JSON file to save your work, or download the Canvas as an image reference for your art.
